Email Templates

Simple reference for what each template type sends.

District

Subject
{MONTH} {YEAR} Financial Reports - {DISTRICT MANAGER}
Attachment Text
{DISTRICT NUMBER} - {DISTRICT MANAGER} ({MONTH} {YEAR})
Body Text
Hi {FIRST NAME}, Attached are the {MONTH} Financial Reports for your District. Please let us know of any questions. Thank you, Yona Finance Team
Example Subject
April 2026 Financial Reports - John Miller
Example Attachment Text
District 101 - John Miller (April 2026)
Example Body Text
Hi John, Attached are the April Financial Reports for your District. Please let us know of any questions. Thank you, Yona Finance Team
